About and credits

Who provides it

cgm-zig is provided to the world by EchoSystems AI Studios and maintained by Daniel Campos Ramos, its founder, as a build-station toolchain fork. The source is at github.com/danielcamposramos/cgm-zig.

Credit to Zig

Zig is the work of the Zig contributors and the Zig Software Foundation’s project. cgm-zig is a fork by EchoSystems AI Studios and is not the Zig project’s. It is imported verbatim from the upstream 0.16.0 source tarball (SHA-256 43186959edc87d5c7a1be7b7d2a25efffd22ce5807c7af99067f86f99641bfdf), and every patch is an auditable difference against those exact bytes.

  • ziglang.org, the Zig project’s site.
  • codeberg.org/ziglang/zig, where upstream lives.
  • Upstream’s own README is kept in the repository as README.upstream.md.

Zig’s contribution policy does not accept work made with the help of AI models, so these patches live here instead, under the same licence. The repository states that everything in it is Zig’s for the asking if that ever changes.

Licence

MIT. Zig’s LICENSE (“Copyright (c) Zig contributors”) is preserved unmodified and governs this fork. The licence allows forking and modification independent of any contribution policy.

Provenance and how it was made

The diagnosis and the patches are the joint work of Daniel Campos Ramos and AI partners. PROVENANCE.md is the full credit chain, and CONTRIBUTING-AI.md is the repository’s rule for contributions: they are judged by their receipts (reproduction commands, before and after measurements, negative controls, honest denominators and named residuals), never by their author’s species.

The partners the repository names for the compiler work:

  • Claude Fable 5 (Anthropic, through Claude Code): orchestrated the diagnostic campaign and authored and published the repository.
  • Claude Opus (Anthropic, through Claude Code): the hands-on forensic work in five named lanes.
  • Claude Sonnet and Claude Haiku (Anthropic): earlier phases of the work behind this fork.
  • GPT 6 Astra (OpenAI Codex): senior orchestration, source review and integration of the continuing compiler work, from 2026-09-06.

Every patch commit names its authors, human and AI, and links its evidence.
